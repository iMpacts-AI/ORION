## 1. Executive Summary

**iMpact** is an independent technology initiative focused on engineering useful, human-centered software and practical intelligent systems. 

Unlike contemporary AI startups that prioritize hyper-inflated marketing claims, viral demos, or speculative tokenomics, iMpact is grounded in a philosophy of **restraint, verifiable implementation, and human sovereignty**:

> *"Software that acts on your behalf should show what it is doing, ask at the right moment, and stop the instant you say so."*

iMpact's primary flagship technology project is **ORION**, a permission-bound desktop AI computer-use agent.

---

## 2. The Four Design Commitments

All software developed by iMpact is held against four explicit commitments:

1. **Useful Before Impressive**: We start from real work and real constraints. If a feature does not make a task shorter, clearer, or calmer, it does not ship.
2. **The Person Stays in Command**: Software that acts on your behalf should show what it is doing, require permission for sensitive actions, and stop the instant you tell it to stop.
3. **Restraint is a Feature**: Fewer surfaces, clearer language, quieter interfaces. Depth is available when needed, never forced upfront.
4. **Built to Last**: We choose durable architectural foundations over quick superficial wins, and we would rather name what is unfinished than imply it is done.

## 4. Website Technical Review (`https://impacts-ai.com`)

An inspection of the live web application at `https://impacts-ai.com` was conducted:

* **Framework & Architecture:** Built with TanStack Router, React, and Tailwind CSS.
* **Typography:** Premium editorial typography using *Instrument Sans*, *Instrument Serif*, *IBM Plex Sans Arabic*, and *JetBrains Mono*.
* **Bilingual Support:** Alternate metadata and language selectors configured for English (`en`) and Arabic (`ar`).
* **Design Restraint:** Clean dark theme (`#141417`), subtle aurora ambient glows, accessible color contrasts, and restrained kinetic text animations.
* **Navigation & Routes:**
  * `/` (Home / Manifest)
  * `/orion` (ORION Product Page)
  * `/pricing` (Transparent Early Access Gating)
  * `/roadmap` (Public Development Targets)
  * `/blog` (Journal / Technical Reflections)
  * `/about` (Company Philosophy & Mission)
  * `/contact` (Early Access Request Form)
  * `/legal` (`/terms`, `/privacy`, `/refund-policy`, `/cookies`)
* **Integrity Audit:** Zero false claims of revenue, enterprise customers, or fabricated awards. The site clearly and honestly states: *"Nothing on this page describes a shipped, generally available feature."*
